Crowned A4 Portrait 3×2
Cut six cards from the top two rows of a portrait A4 sheet

1. Download and open the Silhouette Card Maker A4 Standard Borderless v2
   Studio template. It contains the matching portrait page and registration marks.
   https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Alan-Cha/silhouette-card-maker/0da09b729814aa5aafde024b5a6059624fa87951/cutting_templates/borderless/a4-standard-borderless-v2.studio3
2. Save a copy for Portrait 3×2. Delete only the three lowest card cut paths.
   Leave the remaining six positions, page, mat and registration settings as saved.
   The PDF and cut sheet still use all three marks, including the mark near the
   bottom-left corner. Match the mat orientation shown in Studio.
3. If importing the DXF instead, retain the saved v2 page and registration setup
   and replace its cut paths. The DXF has six closed rounded rectangles in mm.
   Relative to the top-left edge of the PHYSICAL A4 sheet, the group is
   191.516 mm wide and 177.207 mm tall, at X 9.229 mm and Y 15.240 mm.
   Use that physical origin even if the saved Studio page has an offset.
4. Print at actual size. The Epson receives portrait A4 through its Rear Paper
   Feeder. This layout keeps the bottom card row free of artwork while preserving
   the page marks. Set finished fronts aside flat to dry.
5. If there are backs, flip around the portrait long edge and feed the same
   short edge first. Print only the matching packet's backs from Printer in CLC.
   Confirm feed direction and front/back alignment on a single proof sheet first.
6. Verify registration and all six cut positions on a proof sheet before
   sending a batch. The printed card cuts are approximately 63 × 88 mm.
